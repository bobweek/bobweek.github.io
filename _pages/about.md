I apply mathematical and computational approaches to study the dynamics of biological populations. My background is in applying stochastic processes to understand interspecfic coevolution. Currently, I am a [KiTE postdoctoral research fellow](https://www.uni-kiel.de/en/research/early-career-researchers/kite) at Kiel University, where I develop theory to understand host-microbiome interactions. In Kiel, I am hosted by the [Schulenburg Group](https://evoecogen-kiel.de/) and the [Collaborative Research Center 1182](https://www.metaorganism-research.com/) focused on the study of metaorganisms. I also hold a guest affiliation with the [Max Planck Institute for Evolutionary Biology](https://www.evolbio.mpg.de/) in Plön, where I collaborate with [Amanda de Azevedo-Lopes](https://www.evolbio.mpg.de/person/123299/15303) and [Arne Traulsen](https://www.evolbio.mpg.de/person/12087/16397) in the Department of Theoretical Biology.

My research also includes ongoing collaborations with the [Bohannan Group](https://pages.uoregon.edu/bohannanlab/) at the University of Oregon, [Aura Raulo](https://www.biology.ox.ac.uk/people/aura-raulo) at Oxford, [Lisandro Milocco](https://lisandromilocco.github.io/) at Stockholm University and SciLifeLab, and [Luke Harmon](https://lukejharmon.github.io/) at the University of Idaho.
